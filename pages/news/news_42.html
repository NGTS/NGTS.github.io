Snow at Paranal!

August 2026: We've had some crazy weather at Paranal! Usually the area gets below 10 mm of rain annually, but we've just had ~15 mm of rain and SNOW over a couple of nights, due to a supercharged El Niño. Sadly NGTS could not observe in these conditions, but we did get some spectacular views of the Atacama desert blanketed in snow! Check out this video of the conditions over the course of a night...!.
