Multi-Planet Systems Conference, Sofia 2026

September 2026:Last week (31st Aug - 4th Sept) some of the NGTS consortium members visited St Kliment Ohridski (Sofia University) in Bulgaria for a conference called EXO-MPS 2026 all about systems with multiple planets! Some exciting topics covered included: detecting multi-planet systems with transits and radial velocities, planet formation and migration theory, transit timing variations, resonant systems, and planets around binary stars. NGTS featured in talks from Sam Gill (a three planet system with NGTS), Ben Davies (circumbinary planets), Solène Ulmer-Moll (following up TESS multi-planet systems), and Dan Bayliss (connecting planets from TESS, NGTS, and Gaia). It was great for NGTS members from different institutes to get together, and also to meet with researchers from across the globe working on multi-planet systems! A great week in a beautiful city! Huge thank you to Trifon Trifonov and the EXO-RESTART team for a wonderful, insightful conference..
News item author: Amber Sedgley, University of Warwick
