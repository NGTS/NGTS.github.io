The Venue: Sabhal Mòr Ostaig

The Conference will be taking place at Scotlands's national centre for Gaelic language and culture; Sabhal Mòr Ostaig. Located on Skye's Sleat Peninsula, the campus offers outstanding views across the spectacular Sound of Sleat shoreline.

Food and accommodation will be provided on site and their costs incorporated into the registration fee. For accomodation either single or twin bed rooms are available and the canteen caters to a wide range of dietary requirements. Arrangements can be made to extend your stay before/after the conference on an individual basis and at your own expense.

If you are seeking alternative accommodation please inform the LOC. There are hotels nearby, however these are likely to be both expensive and busy at the time of the conference. Camping is also allowed onsite and there are 24hr showers and toilets available.

For more information on Sabhal Mòr Ostaig, please see their website: www.smo.uhi.ac.uk
