The Venue: Sabhal Mòr Ostaig

The Conference will be taking place at Scotlands's national centre for Gaelic language and culture; Sabhal Mòr Ostaig. Located on Skye's Sleat Peninsula, the campus offers outstanding views across the spectacular Sound of Sleat shoreline.

Food and accomodation will be provided on site and their costs incorporated into the registration fee. For accomodation either single or twin bed rooms are available and the canteen caters to a wide range of dietary requirements.

For more information on Sabhal Mòr Ostaig, please see their website: www.smo.uhi.ac.uk
